# Future Company Partnership Models (Ethics-Preserving)

> **Status:** Exploratory (post–Phase 5)  
> **Intent:** Capture partnership ideas that support users in hiring contexts  
> **Constraint:** Must preserve user agency, dignity, and epistemic safety

This document collects **potential future directions** for partnering with companies **without** selling user psychological data, scores, or inferred traits.

These ideas are intentionally deferred until after Phase 5 is complete and validated.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **Companies may observe work, thinking, and artifacts —  
> but must never possess or interpret the person.**

Users remain the owners, narrators, and gatekeepers of their information at all times.

---

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

These apply to all future partnership models:

- ❌ No standardized psychological tests as system inputs
- ❌ No personality scores, rankings, or classifications
- ❌ No selling or transferring user psychological or cognitive data
- ❌ No employer dashboards aggregating user traits
- ❌ No hidden inference layers for employers
- ❌ No coercive “consent” tied to employability

Disagreement and user self-authorship must remain first-class.

---

## Model 1: Portfolio of Thinking (User-Owned, Employer Read-Only)

### Concept
Users curate a **shareable portfolio of thinking**, composed of:
- selected prompt responses
- project narratives
- self-authored reflections explaining *why* an artifact matters

The AI assists only with:
- clarity
- structure
- narrative coherence

The system never labels, scores, or interprets the user.

### Employer Interaction
- Employers view only what the user explicitly shares
- No analytics, no hidden signals
- Interpretation is entirely human

### Why This Fits
- Demonstrates thinking in action
- Reduces résumé theater
- Preserves agency and reversibility

---

## Model 2: Interview Readiness as a Sponsored Product

### Concept
Companies sponsor **preparation tracks**, not evaluation.

Examples:
- System design interview prep
- Product sense prompts
- Behavioral clarity rehearsal

Company branding appears as:
> “This preparation track was supported by Company X.”

### Key Rule
- **No user data is sent back to the company**

### Why This Fits
- Companies benefit from better-prepared candidates
- Users feel supported, not judged
- No power asymmetry or surveillance

---

## Model 3: Signals Without Scores (User-Curated Capabilities)

### Concept
Users choose to display **self-asserted capabilities**, such as:
- Comfort with ambiguity
- Systems thinking
- Cross-functional collaboration

Each capability links to:
- specific prompt responses
- projects
- user-written explanations

The system helps align evidence to claims but never verifies or assigns signals.

### Key Rule
> The system never decides whether a signal is true —  
> it only helps the user support what they choose to claim.

---

## Model 4: Company-Authored Prompts (User-Owned Answers)

### Concept
Companies publish prompts that reflect how they think, such as:
> “Describe how you approach ambiguous requirements.”

Users may:
- answer privately
- reuse across applications
- selectively share responses

### Employer Interaction
- Employers see only what users opt to share
- No hidden analysis
- No access to broader user history

### Why This Fits
- Pull-based, not extractive
- Helps candidates self-select
- Encourages transparency without coercion

---

## Model 5: Hiring Confidence, Not Hiring Data

### Concept
Focus monetization on **user capability building**, independent of employers:
- narrative coaching
- interview rehearsal
- clarity about strengths and preferences
- resume-to-story alignment

Employers benefit indirectly from clearer, more confident candidates.

### Why This Fits
- Avoids ethical and legal risk
- Preserves trust
- Aligns with system’s reflective nature

---

## What This System Will Never Become

To preserve trust and epistemic safety, this system will not become:

- a background check
- a psychometric assessment platform
- a hiring filter or ranking engine
- a predictive scoring system
- a proxy evaluator for employers

---

## Timing and Scope

These models are **explicitly post–Phase 5**.

Current priorities remain:
- Phase 5.5 consent UI
- memory ledger transparency
- hard boundary enforcement
- “never allowed to say” constraints

This document exists to preserve intent, not accelerate implementation.

---

## Closing Note

The long-term value of this system depends on **trust**.

Trust is built by:
- keeping users in control
- resisting over-instrumentation
- refusing extractive shortcuts

Any future partnership must strengthen, not erode, that foundation.
